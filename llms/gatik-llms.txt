# Gatik

> Gatik runs autonomous trucks for Fortune 50 retailers and grocers, moving freight between distribution centers and stores every day in North America.

Gatik is an autonomous trucking company focused on the middle mile: repeatable, short-haul freight routes between hubs and retail endpoints. The company deploys Gatik Driver™, an AI-powered autonomy system built for commercial operations.
